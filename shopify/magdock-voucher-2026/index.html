Consent and sending

  • The tag is set by purchase, not by newsletter subscription. Shopify consent and Brevo consent are separate.
  • Buyers were compared with the recipients of both Insider newsletters (Brevo exports) and Shopify marketing consent.
  • Brevo: list "MagDock Insider 2026" (#31) with 64 contacts → 50 recipients; 14 are blocklisted in Brevo and are skipped automatically.
  • Outlook: the 14 non-subscribers get a short personal email (BCC) with voucher information only, no other advertising.
  • Before sending: remove the 2 redeemed customers and the late order from the list; Patricia decides on dealers, late order and shipping (€5.90 if only the MagDock is ordered).
