Shopify dealer orders: payment terms from the customer

Why dealer invoices were “due immediately”, what the Shopify connector does, and the automation rule that gives Hundesport Kallinger 15 days.

← Odoo customizations
Prepared by: Hamza Mohsin · Done: 12.11.2026 · Live in production · Patricia point 2 (weekly check 11.11.2026)

1. Why

Patricia's weekly check of 11.11.2026, point 2: dealer invoices were due immediately although the dealer has payment terms in Shopify. Examples: RE/2026/05772, RE/2026/05406 (Hundesport Kallinger), RE/2026/05020 (CaniTess). The invoice PDF says “Sofortige Zahlung”, and Odoo shows the invoice as overdue from day 1. That makes open items and reminders wrong.

2. Debugging: what was found

CheckResult
Shopify order #IQD-99640 (Kallinger) → Timeline“New payment terms were added”: Net 30, due 10.12.2026, status Payment pending. Shopify was correct.
Odoo sales order #IQD-99640 → Other Info / Shopify InfoPayment Gateway no_payment_gateway, Workflow “Automatic Validation for Manual Payment”, Payment Terms Immediate Payment
Shopify → Configuration → Financial Status (connector)All 21 lines for no_payment_gateway have Payment Term = Immediate Payment. The connector always takes the term from this table and ignores the Shopify payment terms and the customer's own term.
Financial Status: clear the Payment Term (staging test)Not possible: the field is required (“Missing required fields”).
Shopify → Customers → CompaniesB2B companies have different terms: Kallinger Net 30, CaniTess and yakkihundsport “Due on fulfillment”, K9 Norway and Edog Kalypsoland none.
CaniTess order #IQD-98953 (RE/2026/05020)Payment terms Due on fulfillment, so Odoo matches Shopify here. Not a technical error; whether CaniTess gets more days is a business decision.
Odoo contact Bruno FluriSALES Payment Terms = “Zahlbar sofort ohne Abzug” (immediate)
Odoo payment terms list10, 21, 30, 45 Days etc. existed, no 15 Days
Staging → Shopify → Configuration → InstancesStaging is connected to the live store (iqdogsport.myshopify.com), not the test store. Test-store orders cannot reach staging; do not run Import Orders in staging.
Root cause: the Emipro connector sets the order's payment term from its own Financial Status table (gateway + financial status + order status). For no_payment_gateway that is always “Immediate Payment”. The dealer's terms from Shopify or from the Odoo contact are never used.

Decision (Florian, 12.11.2026)

Hundesport Kallinger gets 15 days (not 30). Changed by Hamza in Shopify on the company and its location (both Net 15).

3.3 Automation rule

FieldValue
WhereSettings → Technical → Automation → Automation Rules
NameIQ – Payment term from customer for unpaid Shopify orders
Model / TriggerSales Order (sale.order) / On create
Apply on[("shopify_instance_id", "!=", False), ("shopify_payment_gateway_id.name", "=", "no_payment_gateway")]
ActionExecute Code
StatusStaging: created 12.11.2026 · Production: live 12.11.2026
for order in records:
    term = order.partner_id.commercial_partner_id.property_payment_term_id
    if term and order.payment_term_id != term:
        old = order.payment_term_id.name or "-"
        order.write({"payment_term_id": term.id})
        order.message_post(body="Payment term set from customer: %s (was: %s)" % (term.name, old))

What the rule does and does not do

New Shopify order with no_payment_gatewayResult
Customer company has a SALES payment term (Kallinger = 15 Days)Order gets that term + chatter note; the invoice created from the order gets it too
Customer without a termNo change, stays Immediate Payment
Customer with “Zahlbar sofort ohne Abzug” (e.g. Bruno Fluri)Gets that term instead of “Immediate Payment” (same meaning, different text)

Not affected: existing orders and invoices, manual Odoo orders, orders paid by card / PayPal / Vorkasse / COD (other gateways), contacts.

5. How to undo

  1. Rule: Automation Rules → “IQ – Payment term from customer…” → archive.
  2. Contact: Kallinger → SALES → Payment Terms back to “Zahlbar sofort ohne Abzug”.

The payment term “15 Days” can stay; it does nothing unless it is used.

6. Next: automatic payment reminders (Florian)

Florian's request: remind unpaid customers automatically and notify Florian and Patricia if still unpaid. Findings 12.11.2026 (production and staging are the same):

  • Accounting → Configuration → Follow-up Levels: two levels, 15 Days and 30 Days after due date, Email on, Automatic off. That is why no reminders go out.
  • Templates “Payment Reminder” and “Second reminder followup” are Odoo's default English texts (“Pay now” button, 8 days).
  • Proposed rule (to confirm with Florian): due date friendly email · +7 official reminder · +14 second reminder · +21 To-Do for Patricia/Florian.
  • Before switching on Automatic: check the 26 late invoices (paid in Shopify but not matched = no reminder).
  • Shopify also offers its own payment reminders for orders with payment terms (alternative to compare).
